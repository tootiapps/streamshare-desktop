# Privacy policy

StreamShare TV Client does not include analytics, advertising, telemetry or
automatic crash reporting. It does not send account details, playback history
or diagnostics to TootiApps.

The client communicates only as required for its user-requested purpose:

- it announces its availability to devices on the local network;
- it accepts pairing and playback commands from controllers authorized by the
  user;
- it requests media and subtitle URLs supplied through an authorized playback
  command;
- it serves temporary compatibility streams only through the local loopback
  interface.

Pairing credentials and settings are stored locally. Diagnostic information
remains on the device unless the user explicitly copies and shares it. Media
providers, operating-system vendors and download platforms may apply their own
privacy policies to services that users choose to access.

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the repository's issue
tracker without including credentials, media URLs or other sensitive data.
